# Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) Model – 5 Years

**Date of Preparation:** July 10, 2026  
**Purpose:** Comparing the true cost of vendors after adding all expected hidden fees.

---

## 1. Executive Summary of Results (The Surprise)

| Vendor | Initial Cost (Sales Pitch) | Cost After Hidden Fees | Inflation (%) | Final Rank |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Vendor (A)** | 58,300,000 | 74,200,000 | +27% | 2nd |
| **Vendor (B)** | 48,500,000 | 56,750,000 | +17% | **1st (Winner)** |
| **Vendor (C)** | 36,500,000 | 69,850,000 | +91% | 3rd (Rejected) |

> **Key Finding:** Vendor (C), which was 37% cheaper than Vendor (B), becomes 23% more expensive after hidden fees are applied. Vendor (B) is the most transparent and the lowest overall cost.

## 3. Financial Recommendation

Based on the TCO analysis:
- **Vendor (C) is immediately disqualified** due to the astronomical actual cost and lack of transparency.
- **Vendor (B) is recommended as the primary choice**, with a negotiating condition to reduce the maintenance rate from 20% to 17%, and freeze Sandbox fees for the first 3 years.
- **Vendor (A) is kept as a secondary option**, on the condition that they are forced to include all modules in the base price as a prerequisite for approval.
