## 3. Strategic Mitigation Matrix (Negotiation Countermeasures)

| Strategic Trap | Mitigation Action (Negotiation Solution) | Proposed Contractual Wording |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Unbundling** | Force vendor to provide All-Inclusive Licensing covering all current/future modules. | *"The Vendor agrees that the base license includes all functional modules (including Islamic, Corporate, and Treasury) with no additional future fees."* |
| **Excessive Maintenance** | Cap maintenance at 17% and freeze it for 5 years. | *"Annual maintenance fees are fixed at 17% of the license value, with no increases during the contract term."* |
| **Forced Upgrades** | Make upgrades optional, delink them from support, and fix upgrade pricing. | *"Upgrades are optional and not required for support continuity. The price for any major upgrade is fixed at (X) USD."* |
| **SI Black Box** | Enforce Joint & Several Liability with the SI partner, making the vendor solely accountable. | *"The Prime Vendor is jointly and severally liable with its SI partner, and bears full responsibility for all deliverables."* |
| **Customization Ransom** | Set a cap on free consulting hours, or offer fixed-price project bids. | *"The Vendor provides 500 free consulting hours annually for minor changes. Any extra work is priced at a pre-agreed fixed rate per project."* |
| **Sandbox Tax** | Demand that Dev/Test environments be included at no extra cost. | *"Provision of development and testing environments is an integral part of this contract, with no additional monthly fees."* |
| **Premium Support** | Demand 24/7 premium support included in the contract, or convert to a fixed annual fee. | *"The contract includes 24/7 technical support with no additional fees, or a fixed annual fee of (X) USD."* |
