---

## 2. Detailed Sub-Criteria

**A. Technical Quality (35 pts):**
- Data Residency Compliance (15 pts) – Knock-out if failed.
- SAMA CSF/CCF Compliance (10 pts).
- System Modernity & Scalability (5 pts).
- Advanced Cybersecurity (SAST/DAST) (5 pts).

**B. TCO (30 pts):**
- Pricing Transparency (10 pts).
- Value vs. Market Average (10 pts).
- Hidden Fee Protection / Change Order limitation (10 pts).

**C. Experience (15 pts):**
- Similar regional projects (10 pts).
- Certifications & Financial Stability (5 pts).

**D. Implementation Plan (10 pts):**
- Plan Clarity & Schedule (5 pts).
- Risk Management & Emergency Plans (5 pts).

**E. Cost Transparency (10 pts):**
- Full Subcontractor Disclosure (5 pts).
- All-Inclusive commitment (5 pts).

---

## 3. Scoring Mechanics

- Minimum passing score: **70/100**, provided the Data Residency condition is fully met.
- In case of a tie, the bid with the higher (Cost Transparency) score prevails.
