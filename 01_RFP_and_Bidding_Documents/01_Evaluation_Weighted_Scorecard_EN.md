# Weighted Evaluation Scorecard – AlVirtu Bank Core Banking Project

**Date of Preparation:** July 10, 2026  
**Document Status:** Confidential (Evaluation Committee Only)

---

## 1. Evaluation Criteria & Weights

| # | Criterion | Weight (%) | Maximum Points (out of 100) |
| :-: | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| 1 | Technical Quality & Regulatory Compliance (SAMA CSF, CCF, PDPL) | 35% | 35 |
| 2 | Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) over 5 Years | 30% | 30 |
| 3 | Vendor Experience & Track Record (Local Presence, Previous Projects) | 15% | 15 |
| 4 | Implementation Plan & Project Management (Schedule, Team, Risks) | 10% | 10 |
| 5 | Cost Structure Transparency (Hidden Fees, Change Orders) | 10% | 10 |
| | **Total** | **100%** | **100** |

---

## 2. Detailed Evaluation (Sub-Criteria)

### A. Technical Quality & Regulatory Compliance (35 Points)

| Sub-Criterion | Points | Description |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Data Residency Compliance** | 15 | If the proposal shows any gap in localization, it is immediately disqualified. |
| **Compliance with SAMA CSF & CCF** | 10 | Adherence to all mandatory controls. |
| **System Modernity & Scalability** | 5 | Support for Microservices, APIs, and integration with national systems. |
| **Advanced Cybersecurity (SAST/DAST)** | 5 | Provision of continuous SAST/DAST and an Incident Response Plan. |

---

### B. Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) – 30 Points

| Sub-Criterion | Points | Description |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Pricing Transparency** | 10 | Clarity of all cost items (Licenses, Maintenance, Training, Integration). |
| **Added Value vs. Market Average** | 10 | Comparison of total cost with market averages ($50M-$120M). |
| **Hidden Fee Protection** | 10 | Existence of clauses preventing random Change Orders and fixing developer rates for 5 years. |

---

### C. Vendor Experience & Track Record – 15 Points

| Sub-Criterion | Points | Description |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Similar Projects in the Region** | 10 | Execution of at least 3 Core Banking projects in KSA or the Gulf. |
| **Certifications & Financial Stability** | 5 | ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II, and financial reports proving stability. |

---

### D. Implementation Plan & Project Management – 10 Points

| Sub-Criterion | Points | Description |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Clarity of Plan & Timeline** | 5 | Details of phases, tasks, and resources. |
| **Risk Management & Contingency Plans** | 5 | Existence of an approved (Rollback) and Disaster Recovery plan. |

---

### E. Cost Structure Transparency – 10 Points

| Sub-Criterion | Points | Description |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Full Disclosure of Subcontractors** | 5 | Disclosure of all subcontractors and their data storage locations. |
| **Commitment to Preventing Hidden Fees** | 5 | Provision of a signed declaration that all required features are included in the base price (All-Inclusive). |

---

## 3. Final Scoring Mechanics

- Total points for each bidder are summed (out of 100).
- A bid is considered **Successful** if it achieves at least **70 points**, provided the Data Residency criterion is fully met.
- In the event of a tie, the bidder with the higher score in (Cost Transparency) prevails.

---

## 4. Evaluation Committee Powers

- The Committee has the right to request Bid Clarifications from vendors.
- The Committee has the right to exclude any proposal proven to contain misleading or omitted information.
- The final recommendation will be submitted to the Senior Steering Committee for final approval.
