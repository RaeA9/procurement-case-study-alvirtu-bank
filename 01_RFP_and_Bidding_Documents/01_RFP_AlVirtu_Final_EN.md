# RFP – Core Banking System Replacement (AlVirtu Bank)

**RFP No.:** RFP-AlVirtu-2026-001  
**Date:** July 10, 2026  
**Status:** Official (Ready for Vendor Distribution)

---

## 1. Executive Summary

AlVirtu Bank seeks to replace its legacy Core Banking System, which currently hinders growth, innovation, and regulatory compliance. This project aims to enhance operational efficiency, enable advanced digital services, and ensure full compliance with SAMA and SDAIA regulations.

**Estimated Project Value:** $50M – $120M USD (Including Licensing, Implementation, 5-year Maintenance, and Integration).

---

## 2. Project Scope

**Functional Scope:**
- Account Management (Current, Savings, Investment, Multi-currency).
- Financing & Lending (Retail, Corporate, Islamic).
- Payments & Transfers (Local/International, SADAD, SARIE, SWIFT).
- Cards Management (Debit, Credit, Pre-paid).
- Digital Services (Open Banking APIs, Mobile App).
- AML/CFT and real-time Regulatory Reporting.

**Technical Scope:**
- 15,000 TPS with < 150ms response time.
- 99.999% Availability with DRP.
- Hybrid Infrastructure (On-Prem + Cloud) with 100% Data Residency in KSA.
- Comprehensive Encryption (AES-256, TLS 1.3) and IAM/MFA.

**Service Scope:**
- Training for 500+ employees and full Knowledge Transfer.
- 24/7 Support with on-site engineers in Riyadh during launch.
- 5-year Warranty with monthly security patches.

---

## 3. Mandatory Compliance Requirements (SAMA & SDAIA)

A. Full compliance with SAMA CSF & CCF.  
B. Full compliance with SDAIA PDPL.  
C. 100% Data Residency for ALL environments.  
D. Monthly SAST/DAST reports and closure of Critical/High vulnerabilities before Go-Live.

---

## 4. Knock-out Criterion (Immediate Disqualification)

> **"Data Sovereignty Clause:** The Bidder must explicitly confirm that ALL data (including backups and logs) for ALL environments will be processed and stored exclusively within KSA. Any evidence to the contrary in the proposal will result in immediate disqualification without further review."
