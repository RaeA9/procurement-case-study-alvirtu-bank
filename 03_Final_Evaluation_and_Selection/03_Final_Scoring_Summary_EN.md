## 3. Decision Rationale (Strategic Analysis)

- **Vendor (B) proved to be the most balanced option**, achieving the highest scores in technical quality and transparency, and the lowest cost inflation (17% vs. 27% and 91% for the others).
- **Vendors (A) and (C) were disqualified by the immediate Knock-out Criterion** due to storing data outside the Kingdom in development and testing environments, which constitutes a clear violation of SAMA's Cloud Computing Framework.
- **Additionally, Vendor (C) failed to meet the minimum required points (70)**, confirming that their offer is technically unqualified.
