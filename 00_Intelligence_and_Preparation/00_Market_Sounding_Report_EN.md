# Market Sounding Report – Core Banking Systems (Saudi Market)

**Prepared by:** Strategic Procurement Team  
**Date:** July 10, 2026  
**Classification:** Confidential (Internal Use Only before RFP Issuance)

---

## 1. Executive Summary (Pricing Calibration)

Based on the nature of the Saudi and Gulf market, and the size of AlVirtu Bank, the estimated budget for the total project (Licenses, Implementation, 5-year Maintenance, and Integration) ranges between **$50M – $120M USD**. This wide range is attributed to:
- Costs of locally licensed cloud providers (which charge a premium for localization).
- Costs of System Integrator consulting firms with local expertise.
- Complex Sharia-compliant product customization requirements.
- Costs of integration with existing national infrastructure.

---

## 2. Analysis of Major Vendors

**Vendor (A) – Traditional Leader:**
- **Market Share:** Very high in major regional banks.
- **Cost Estimate:** $55M – $85M.
- **Reputation:** Technically strong, notorious for complex contracts and extra charges for additional modules.
- **Strategic Trap:** Prone to the "Unbundling Trap." Must force (All-Inclusive Licensing) covering all current and future features (Islamic, Corporate, Retail, Treasury).

**Vendor (B) – Flexible & Moderate:**
- **Market Share:** Strong in medium and large banks.
- **Cost Estimate:** $50M – $95M.
- **Reputation:** Highly flexible, but requires strict governance over System Integrator (SI) costs.
- **Strategic Trap:** Requires (Joint & Several Liability) to make the primary vendor the single point of accountability for the entire project.

**Vendor (C) – Economical Aggressor:**
- **Market Share:** Strong contender, but less local presence.
- **Cost Estimate:** $45M – $75M.
- **Reputation:** Tempting low licensing price, but very expensive customization costs.
- **Strategic Trap:** Same "Unbundling" and exorbitant customization fees.

**Vendor (D) – Cloud-Native (Modern Disruptor):**
- **Pros:** Speed, flexibility, lower initial licensing.
- **Cost Estimate:** $40M – $65M.
- **Challenge:** Scarcity of local experts, weak Arabic documentation.
- **Recommendation:** Include as a Benchmark option in the RFP for discovery.

---

## 3. Hidden & Additional Costs

A. National Systems Integration: $5M – $10M.  
B. Sharia Customization: 15-20% of licensing value.  
C. Training & Knowledge Transfer: Minimum $3M.  
D. Exit Strategy (Migration to new vendor post-contract): Up to $10M.

---

## 4. Critical Tender Clause (Knock-out Criterion – to be inserted in RFP)

> **"Geographic Sovereignty & Compliance Clause (Knock-out Criterion):**  
> The Bidder irrevocably commits that all data for ALL environments (Production, UAT, Dev, DR, Testing) will be stored, processed, and managed exclusively within the geographical borders of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, via locally licensed cloud providers. Any explicit or implicit indication in the technical or financial proposal suggesting data transfer outside the Kingdom will result in immediate disqualification of the entire bid, without technical or financial evaluation."
