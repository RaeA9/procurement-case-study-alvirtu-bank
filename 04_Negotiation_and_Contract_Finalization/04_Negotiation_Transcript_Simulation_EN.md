# Negotiation Transcript Simulation – Final Session with Vendor (B)

**Date (Simulated):** July 20, 2026  
**Venue:** AlVirtu Bank Meeting Room – Riyadh  
**Attendees:** Bank Team (Procurement Manager, General Counsel, CISO, CFO) / Vendor Team (Regional Director, Legal Counsel, Project Manager)

---

## 1. Opening (Declaration of Intent)

**Procurement Manager (Bank):**  
"Thank you for attending. After a comprehensive evaluation, we are pleased to announce that you are the sole finalist for this project. However, there are pricing and contractual gaps that must be closed before we proceed to final signing. We will not waste time; we will move directly to the outstanding points."

**Regional Director (Vendor):**  
"We are delighted with your trust. We are ready to discuss any details to ensure a successful partnership."

---

## 2. Round 1 (Financial Confrontation – Maintenance & Sandbox)

**CFO (Bank):**  
"Your base proposal contains a 20% annual maintenance rate and monthly Sandbox (testing environment) fees of $50,000. Our TCO model shows that this inflates your total cost by 17% compared to the market average. Can you reduce maintenance to 17% and eliminate Sandbox fees entirely?"

**Regional Director (Vendor):**  
"20% is the global minimum for this type of system. Sandbox fees are an operational cost strictly imposed on us by the local cloud provider. We cannot waive them."

**Procurement Manager (Bank):**  
"Allow me to correct you. The market average in the region is 17%. We have received an offer from Vendor (A) with 18% maintenance and unlimited free Sandboxes. You are not the only player in the market. Regarding the environments, competitors include them for free. If you insist on these terms, we will be forced to re-open the tender with Vendor (A), after obliging them to modify their offer to include full Data Residency."

**Legal Counsel (Vendor):**  
*(After a brief internal discussion)* "We can offer a middle ground: reduce maintenance to 18%, and reduce Sandbox fees to $15,000 per month."

**CFO (Bank):**  
"We propose 17% maintenance and $0 for environments, with these rates fixed for 5 years. This is our final offer on this point."

*(Short silence)*  
**Regional Director (Vendor):** "We accept 17.5% maintenance and $0 for environments, provided the contract term is extended to 7 years instead of 5."

**Procurement Manager (Bank):** "Accepted. We will extend the contract to 7 years in exchange for your commitment to these rates."

---

## 3. Round 2 (Legal Engagement – Joint & Several Liability)

**General Counsel (Bank):**  
"You will be using a local System Integrator (SI) partner. We categorically reject the 'finger-pointing' model. We demand a Joint & Several Liability contract, making you the first and final point of accountability to us for any implementation failure, whether caused by you or your SI partner."

**Legal Counsel (Vendor):**  
"This is unconventional in our standard contracts. We do not bear liability for our partners' mistakes; that is their role. We can offer a separate bank performance guarantee from them, but not full joint liability."

**Procurement Manager (Bank):**  
"We are contracting with YOU, not your partner. If your partner fails, we have two options: file separate lawsuits against both of you, which is costly, or go directly to arbitration with you. If you trust your partner's competency, why hesitate to stand with them jointly? This raises questions about your partner's quality."

**Regional Director (Vendor):**  
*(After hesitation)* "Alright, we accept joint liability, provided the SI partner is from our approved list, and you grant us the right to replace them if proven negligent, subject to your prior approval."

**General Counsel (Bank):** "Accepted. We will include this clause in the contract."

---

## 4. Round 3 (Final Pressure – Fixed Man-Day Rates)

**Procurement Manager (Bank):**  
"Final point. For future Change Orders, you charge a daily rate (Man-Day Rate) for developers. We want this rate fixed in the contract for the next five years, capped at $1,400 per day."

**Regional Director (Vendor):**  
"This is extremely difficult. Salary costs change annually. We cannot freeze it with such rigidity."

**CISO (Bank):**  
"We know the market average is $1,200 – $1,300 per day. We are offering $1,400 as a generous ceiling. If you do not agree, we will resort to fixed-price bidding for each sub-project, which will be significantly higher than $1,400 per day. The choice is yours."

**Project Manager (Vendor):** *(Whispers to his director)* "He is right. Fixed-price for changes will be very hard to justify to our management. It is better to accept the fixed rate."

**Regional Director (Vendor):** "We accept fixing $1,400 per day for 5 years, with a condition for an annual review subject to negotiation (not mandatory), based on global inflation indices."

**Procurement Manager (Bank):** "Accepted, provided the review is optional solely at the bank's discretion."

---

## 5. Closing and Finalization

**Procurement Manager (Bank):**  
"Excellent. All outstanding points have been resolved. Our legal team will draft the final contract within 48 hours and send it to you for final review. We expect to sign next week."

**Regional Director (Vendor):**  
"We are pleased with this partnership. We await the final contract."
