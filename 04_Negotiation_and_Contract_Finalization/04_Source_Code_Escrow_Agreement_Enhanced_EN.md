# Source Code Escrow Agreement (Enhanced Version)

**Between:** (AlVirtu Bank), (Vendor B), and (Local Escrow Agent – to be named later)

**Date of Agreement:** [To be determined]  
**Reference:** Appendix (B) of Master Contract C-AlVirtu-2026-001

---

## 1. Subject of the Agreement

This agreement aims to protect AlVirtu Bank from the risks of the Vendor ceasing operations (bankruptcy, business suspension, or cessation of technical support) by depositing the Source Code and all necessary tools to run the system with a neutral third party within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

---

## 2. Contents of the Deposit (What will be deposited)

The Vendor shall deposit the complete and updated version of:

2.1. **Full Source Code:** For all core system components and all custom developments specific to the Bank.

2.2. **Build Tools:** All scripts, environments, and configurations necessary to compile, deploy, and operate the source code into a fully functional system.

2.3. **Third-Party Dependencies:** A complete list of all external libraries and software modules used, along with proof of appropriate licensing.

2.4. **Installation & Configuration Documentation:** Comprehensive installation manuals, configuration guides, and database schema documentation.

---

## 3. Release Conditions (When the Bank may retrieve the code)

The Escrow Agent shall release the deposited materials to the Bank (without requiring the Vendor's consent) in any of the following cases:

1. **Vendor Bankruptcy:** A court judgment declaring the Vendor bankrupt or ceasing its commercial activities.
2. **Cessation of Technical Support:** The Vendor fails to provide the contracted technical support for a period of 60 consecutive days without a valid justification.
3. **Material Data Security Breach:** In the event of proven violation of the Data Residency clause (Clause 1.2 of the Master Contract), which exposes the Bank to immediate regulatory sanctions.

---

## 4. Escrow Agent (Neutral Local Entity)

4.1. The Escrow Agent shall be a neutral, independent, and licensed entity within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, approved by the National Cybersecurity Authority and recognized by SAMA.

4.2. Both parties shall agree on the specific Escrow Agent within 30 days of signing the Master Contract. The choice shall be an integral part of this agreement.

---

## 5. Deposit Updates

5.1. The Vendor is obligated to update the deposit with the Escrow Agent with every Major Release and every significant security update, with a maximum period of 6 months between updates.

5.2. The Escrow Agent shall provide a quarterly report to both the Bank and the Vendor confirming the integrity and completeness of the deposited materials.

---

## 6. Confidentiality of the Deposit

6.1. The Escrow Agent shall maintain the strict confidentiality of the Source Code and Build Tools, and shall not disclose them to any party without a direct Saudi court order, or a joint written request from both the Bank and the Vendor.
