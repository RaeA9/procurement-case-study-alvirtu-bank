## 1. Scope of Work

1.1. The Vendor shall provide the Bank with a fully integrated Core Banking System covering all required modules (Accounts, Lending, Payments, Treasury, Islamic Banking, and Digital Services) as per the specifications detailed in Appendix (A) of this contract.

1.2. **Critical Condition (Data Residency):** The Vendor and all its Subcontractors commit to storing and processing all data (including backups and audit logs) exclusively within locally licensed data centers located in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, for ALL environments (Production, UAT, Dev, Testing, DR). Any breach of this condition constitutes a Material Breach, granting the Bank the right to terminate the contract immediately and recover all paid amounts.

---

## 2. Vendor Obligations

2.1. **System Integrator (SI) Partner:** The Vendor shall provide an approved System Integrator (SI). The Vendor bears full **Joint and Several Liability** for all deliverables of its SI partner, including quality, schedule adherence, and any damages resulting from their negligence.

2.2. **Training & Knowledge Transfer:** The Vendor shall provide a minimum of 500 training hours for Saudi national employees, with a Knowledge Transfer plan approved by the Bank's HR department.

---

## 3. Financial Terms & Pricing

3.1. **Total Estimated Contract Value:** **$56,750,000 USD** (over the entire term, including licensing, implementation, and maintenance).

3.2. **Annual Maintenance Rate:** Fixed at **17.5%** of the base license value, frozen for the full 7-year term, with no annual increases.

3.3. **Testing Environments (Sandbox):** The contract includes the provision of fully mirrored development and testing environments (Dev/Test) with no additional monthly or annual fees.

3.4. **Developer Man-Day Rate:** Fixed at **$1,400 USD** (one thousand four hundred dollars) per day for developers and consultants, locked for the first 5 years (renewable). This rate cannot be amended without the Bank's prior written consent (mandatory, not automatic).

3.5. **Payment Schedule (Milestone-Based):**
- **15%** Advance payment upon signing.
- **25%** upon completion of the Design phase.
- **30%** upon completion of Development and successful UAT (User Acceptance Testing).
- **20%** upon successful Pilot launch.
- **10%** upon Full Go-Live and final system acceptance.

---

## 4. Service Level Agreement (SLA) & Financial Penalties

4.1. **System Uptime:** 99.999% (Five 9s) annually.

4.2. **Incident Response Times:**
- **Critical:** Less than 2 hours.
- **High:** Less than 4 hours.

4.3. **Financial Penalties (Cash, not credits):**
- If system availability drops below 99.9%, a 2% deduction from the annual maintenance payment will be applied.
- If system availability drops below 99%, this will be considered a Material Breach, and the Bank may terminate the contract immediately.

---

## 5. Termination & Exit Strategy

5.1. The Bank may terminate the contract without compensation in the following cases:
- Bankruptcy or cessation of the Vendor's core business operations.
- Breach of the Data Residency clause (Clause 1.2).
- Sustained performance degradation below 95% for 30 consecutive days.

5.2. Upon termination, the Vendor must deliver all Bank data in an Open Format (e.g., CSV, XML) within 30 days, and provide the necessary technical support to migrate it to a new vendor at a fair, pre-agreed rate (or free of charge in the case of termination due to Material Breach by the Vendor).

---

## 6. Dispute Resolution (Arbitration)

6.1. Any disputes shall be referred to the London Court of International Arbitration (LCIA) under English Law, *except* for disputes relating to Data Residency, which shall be exclusively governed by Saudi Law and Saudi Jurisdiction.
