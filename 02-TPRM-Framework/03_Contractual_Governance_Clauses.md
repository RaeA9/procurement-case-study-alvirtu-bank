# Contractual Governance Clauses

**Document ID:** TPRM-CLS-003  
**Institution:** AlVirtu Bank (simulated)  
**Use:** Paste-ready positions for the awarded CBS contract  
**Date:** 1 February 2026  
**Governing law intended:** Laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia  
**Commercial disputes:** Saudi Center for Commercial Arbitration (SCCA), Riyadh

These are drafting positions, not executed terms. Align numbering with the final contract.

---

## 1. Data residency (material term)

The Supplier and every subcontractor shall store and process Bank data — including Production, Development, Test, backup, logs, and disaster-recovery copies — only in facilities located in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and hosted by a provider licensed for that activity in the Kingdom.

A breach of this clause is a **material breach**. The Bank may terminate for cause, recover unearned sums, and claim direct loss within the liability cap. Regulatory matters under SAMA and PDPL stay before Saudi authorities and are not sent to foreign arbitration.

---

## 2. Audit and information rights

On 15 business days’ notice (24 hours if a regulator or a suspected incident so requires) the Bank, its internal audit team, and SAMA or an appointed examiner may inspect controls, locations, and evidence that relate to this service. The Supplier shall make subcontractors available on the same terms.

Cost of a planned annual audit sits with the Bank. Cost of an audit caused by a confirmed control failure sits with the Supplier.

---

## 3. Joint and several liability with the system integrator

The Supplier is jointly and severally liable with its system integrator for delivery, defects, delay, and data incidents arising from the integrator’s work. The Bank may proceed against either party for the whole.

---

## 4. Indemnity (narrow, usable)

The Supplier shall indemnify the Bank against direct loss arising from: (a) a residency breach; (b) a personal-data incident caused by the Supplier or a subcontractor; (c) third-party IP claims against Bank use of the standard product as delivered. Consequential loss stays outside this indemnity except where Saudi mandatory law says otherwise. The liability cap in the main contract applies except for fraud, wilful misconduct, and residency breach to the extent the parties agree a higher sub-cap.

---

## 5. Performance security and insurance

- Performance bond: **10%** of contract value, valid through accepted go-live plus 12 months.
- Advance-payment guarantee: covers the **10%** advance in full.
- Professional indemnity: not less than **USD 10 million** for the term plus three years.

---

## 6. Service credits and termination for service failure

Production availability target: **99.95%** a year.
Each 0.01 percentage point below target: **1%** of annual maintenance, capped at **15%** a year.
Availability below **99.50%** for 30 consecutive days: material breach after 15 days’ notice.

Credits are cash deductions from invoices, not extra service days.

---

## 7. Step-in and exit

If the Supplier is insolvent, or if a material breach is not cured in the notice period, the Bank may step into subcontracted services as needed to keep the platform running.

On any end of term the Supplier shall, within 30 days, deliver Bank data in an open format and Bank-owned configuration. After a material breach, exit support runs 90 days at no extra fee. Product IP stays with the Supplier. Customisations, Bank data, and Bank operating documents vest in the Bank.

---

## 8. Subcontractor control

No subcontractor that will touch Bank data may be added without written Bank consent. Every approved subcontractor is bound to clauses 1, 2, 3 and 7 in writing before work starts.

---

## 9. Continuous assurance

The Supplier shall deliver, each year and on request after a material incident: ISO 27001 currency, SOC 2 Type II or agreed equivalent, SAST/DAST summary for the CBS path, DR test certificate, and a residency attestation listing every site still in use.

Failure to deliver within 30 days of a written request is a material breach.
