# Contract Governance Framework – Long-term Performance & Compliance Management
## AlVirtu Bank – Strategic Vendor Agreement (Vendor B)

**Date of Preparation:** July 10, 2026  
**Document Status:** Official (To be circulated to all relevant committees)  
**Validity Period:** Covers the full contract term (7 Years) plus a 2-year post-termination warranty period.

---

## 1. Core Governance Principle

> **"Transparency and accountability are the twin pillars of any successful strategic partnership."**

This framework aims to ensure:
- The Vendor's full adherence to all contractual, technical, and operational commitments.
- A clear mechanism for reviewing performance and resolving disputes before they escalate.
- The protection of AlVirtu Bank from any deterioration in service quality or unjustified cost overruns.

---

## 2. Joint Governance Structure

| Level | Composition | Frequency | Jurisdiction / Authority |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Joint Steering Committee (JSC)** | (Bank: CEO, COO, CIO, CFO) + (Vendor: Regional Director, VP of Operations) | Quarterly (Every 3 months) | Strategic performance review, approval of additional budgets (if any), resolution of escalated disputes, and evaluation of overall KPIs. |
| **Joint Technical Committee (JTC)** | (Bank: CIO, CISO, Project Manager) + (Vendor: Project Manager, Lead Architect) | Monthly | Review of technical progress, discussion of Change Requests, tracking security vulnerability fixes, and SLA compliance monitoring. |
| **Joint Emergency Team (JET)** | (Bank: CISO, Head of Operations) + (Vendor: Technical Support Director) | On-demand (Convened within 1 hour of incident reporting) | Handling critical security incidents or major outages; coordinating the Rollback Plan if necessary. |

---

## 3. Penalized Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Financial penalties are directly linked to these KPIs (deducted from annual payments) to ensure adherence:

| KPI | Target Value | Penalty for Non-Compliance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **System Uptime (Availability)** | ≥ 99.999% annually | 1% deduction from the annual maintenance fee for every 0.01% drop below the target. |
| **Critical Bug Fix Response Time** | < 4 hours from reporting | $5,000 penalty for each additional hour of delay. |
| **Tier-2 Support Request Response Time** | < 8 business hours | $1,000 penalty for each additional hour of delay. |
| **Monthly Performance Report Accuracy** | 100% (Error-free) | $2,000 penalty for each material error detected. |

---

## 4. Change Management Mechanism

To prevent the recurrence of hidden fee traps, the following process is mandated:

1.  **Change Request Submission:** Initiated by either the Bank or the Vendor via a unified electronic portal.
2.  **Impact Assessment:** The Vendor must provide a technical and financial impact assessment within 5 business days.
3.  **Approval:** No change may be executed without the prior written approval of the Joint Technical Committee (JTC).
4.  **Pricing:** All extra work is billed at the fixed Man-Day Rate ($1,400) as per the contract, with a pre-defined time cap for execution. No additional contingency fees are allowed.

---

## 5. Annual Vendor Performance Review

At the end of each contract year, a comprehensive vendor evaluation is conducted across 4 axes:

1.  **Technical Quality & Compliance (30%):** Adherence to SAMA CSF, PDPL, and security update schedules.
2.  **Schedule Adherence (25%):** Delivery of milestones within the agreed-upon timelines.
3.  **Support Quality & Responsiveness (25%):** Performance of the support team in resolving issues.
4.  **Cost Effectiveness (20%):** Adherence to the agreed budget with no unjustified overruns.

**Result:** If the Vendor scores below 70% in any of these axes, they are given a 30-day improvement period. Failure to improve constitutes a partial material breach.
