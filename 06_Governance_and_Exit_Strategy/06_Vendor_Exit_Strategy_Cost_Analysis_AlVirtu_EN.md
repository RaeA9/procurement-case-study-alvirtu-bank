# Vendor Exit Strategy Cost Analysis – AlVirtu Bank
## Post-7-Year Contract Termination / Vendor Replacement

**Date of Preparation:** July 10, 2026  
**Document Classification:** Top Secret (Negotiation Leverage & Strategic Decision-Making Tool)  
**Purpose:** To calculate the true cost of leaving the current Vendor (Vendor B) and migrating to a new provider, giving the Bank immense negotiating power during contract renewal or re-negotiation.

---

## 1. Executive Summary (Estimated Exit Cost)

> **If AlVirtu Bank decides to leave Vendor (B) after 7 years, the estimated cost of exiting and switching to a new vendor ranges between ($8M – $15M USD).**

This cost is a powerful "pressure card" in negotiations. While the vendor knows the exit is expensive, the Bank knows it is not impossible, creating a balanced power dynamic.

---

---

## 3. Cost Mitigation Factors (How to Reduce Exit Costs)

AlVirtu Bank can significantly reduce this cost by:

1.  **Contract Clause:** Enforcing that the current Vendor must deliver all data in an Open Format at the end of the contract at no additional cost. (This has already been included in the final contract).
2.  **Internal Knowledge Investment:** Investing in in-house (Knowledge Transfer) during the contract term to reduce reliance on external consultants during the migration.
3.  **Flexible APIs:** Developing flexible, well-documented APIs during the contract term to make it easy to connect any new system to the national networks without a full re-engineering effort.

---

## 4. Strategic Recommendation (How to Use This Analysis)

- **In Year 5 of the contract:** Begin renewal (or re-pricing) negotiations with Vendor (B), armed with this analysis. Inform them that you have a credible exit option if they fail to offer improved terms.
- **In Year 6:** Conduct a Market Sounding exercise to evaluate new competitive offers. Compare these offers against the calculated exit cost above. If new offers provide 20-30% operational cost savings, exiting may become a strategically viable decision.
