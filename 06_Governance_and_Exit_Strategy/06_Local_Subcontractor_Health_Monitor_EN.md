# Local Subcontractor Health Monitor – AlVirtu Bank
## Vendor (B)'s Local Implementation Partner (SI)

**Date of Preparation:** July 10, 2026  
**Document Status:** Dynamic (Updated Monthly / Quarterly)  
**Purpose:** To detect any early warning signs that may indicate financial or operational distress of the local partner, potentially impacting project progress and quality.

---

## 1. Core Monitoring Principle

> **"We are not just contracting a company; we are contracting its financial stability and its human capital."**

The local partner is evaluated across 4 main axes. Each axis is scored from (1 to 5), where (5) is Excellent and (1) is Critical.

---

## 2. Quarterly Health Check Axes (Evaluation Criteria)

| Axis | Metrics | Weight | Current Status (Example) |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Financial Stability** | (A) Cash flow trends, (B) Quarterly profits, (C) Pending lawsuits or liens. | 30% | 🟢 Good (4/5) |
| **Employee Turnover Rate** | (A) Resignation rate of key developers/engineers, (B) Speed of replacement. | 25% | 🟡 Medium (3/5) *(Note: Slight increase in resignations observed in the last quarter).* |
| **Delivery Quality** | (A) Number of Critical bugs found in UAT, (B) On-time delivery percentage. | 25% | 🟢 Good (4/5) |
| **Security Compliance** | (A) Adherence to Remote Access policies, (B) Zero security vulnerabilities detected in internal audits. | 20% | 🟢 Good (4/5) |

---

## 3. Final Classification Matrix (Partner Health Status)

| Total Score (Out of 20) | Classification | Required Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **18 – 20** | 🟢 Stable & Excellent | Continue standard monitoring. |
| **14 – 17** | 🟡 Needs Intensive Monitoring | Schedule monthly meetings with the partner's management to discuss early warning indicators. |
| **Below 14** | 🔴 Unstable / High Risk | Activate the Emergency Plan (Replace the partner or expand the Bank's internal team). Notify the Prime Vendor formally. |

---

## 5. Escalation & Powers in Case of (Red - 🔴) Classification

If the partner's classification falls to (Red) for two consecutive quarters, the following protocol is activated:

1.  **Formal Warning:** A written warning is issued to the Prime Vendor (Vendor B) requiring them to replace the partner or submit a corrective action plan within 30 days.
2.  **Activate Joint Liability Clause:** The Bank invokes the Joint & Several Liability clause, demanding the Prime Vendor to provide a replacement implementation team immediately to ensure continuity without operational stoppage.
3.  **Escalation to JSC:** The matter is escalated to the Joint Steering Committee (JSC) for a strategic decision regarding the continuation of the working relationship with the current partner.
