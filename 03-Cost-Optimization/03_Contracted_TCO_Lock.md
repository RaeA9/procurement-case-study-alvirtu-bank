## 1. Two numbers, two jobs

| Number | Job |
| :--- | :--- |
| USD **56.75 million** | Five-year TCO **before** talks. Used to rank bids. |
| USD **51.65 million** | Seven-year **contracted** value **after** talks. Used in the contract and in governance. |

Do not quote 56.75m as the signed price. Do not quote 51.65m as the evaluation TCO.

---

## 2. Bridge

| Line | Pre-deal 5-year book | Contracted 7-year book |
| :--- | ---: | ---: |
| Platform licence | 14.00 | 14.00 |
| Maintenance | 14.00 (20% × 5 years) | 17.15 (17.5% × 7 years) |
| National integration | 4.50 | 4.50 |
| Training / knowledge transfer | 3.00 | 3.00 |
| System integrator | 13.00 | 13.00 |
| Sandbox / Dev-Test | 3.00 | 0 |
| After-hours support | 2.50 | 0 (inside maintenance) |
| Expected light customisation | 2.75 | 0 (500 hours / year included) |
| **Total** | **56.75** | **51.65** |

USD million.

The term got longer. The cash still fell because three leakage lines were killed and the rate dropped 2.5 points.

---

## 3. What must stay locked in the instrument

- Maintenance **17.5%** of the USD 14.00m licence, seven years, no index unless the Bank agrees.
- Man-day **USD 1,400** for the same seven years.
- Environments at **zero** extra fee.
- Advance **10%** against a bank guarantee; performance bond **10%**.
- Availability **99.95%**; credits in cash, cap 15% of annual maintenance.
