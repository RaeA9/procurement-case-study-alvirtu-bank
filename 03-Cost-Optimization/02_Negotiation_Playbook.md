**Institution:** AlVirtu Bank (simulated)  
**Counterparty:** Vendor B (only eligible bidder after residency knock-out)  
**Date:** 3 September 2026

## 1. Mandate

| Item | Position |
| :--- | :--- |
| Walk-away | Sign nothing that leaves Dev/Test or DR outside KSA |
| BATNA | A **rewritten** Vendor A bid: every environment on-kingdom, all-in licence, maintenance ≤ 18%. The original A bid is ineligible |
| Time box | Ten working days from first commercial session |
| Authority | Procurement leads; Compliance holds the residency veto; Finance holds the rate and cash-timing veto |

## 2. Opening book (Vendor B, pre-deal)

Published five-year package USD 48.50m. Hidden-fee TCO USD **56.75m** (+17%), driven by sandbox, after-hours, and light customisation.

That 56.75m number is the **ceiling to beat**, not the opening ask.

## 3. The five asks, in order

| # | Ask | Accept | Stretch | Walk-away |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Maintenance | 17.5% frozen 7 years | 16.5% | Any rate that steps without Bank consent |
| 2 | Sandbox / Dev-Test | USD 0 extra | Same | Monthly environment fee |
| 3 | Man-day | USD 1,400 for **7 years** | 1,300 | Freeze shorter than the contract term |
| 4 | Included hours | 500 customisation hours / year | 750 | Zero included hours |
| 5 | Security package | 10% bond + advance guarantee + PI ≥ USD 10m | Higher PI | Unsecured 15% advance |

Do not trade residency, joint liability with the SI, or audit rights against a cheaper licence.

## 4. Sequence in the room

1. Confirm Section B in writing before price.
2. Put the 56.75m TCO on the table. Ask which lines die.
3. Lock rate and inclusion. Only then discuss term (5 → 7 years).
4. Close cash timing and bonds.
5. Read back the clause pack (TPRM-CLS-003) and stop.

## 5. If they stall

Return to BATNA. Do not reopen Vendor C. Do not score an off-kingdom “temporary” Dev site.

Outcome of the simulated session: asks 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 accepted. Term extended to seven years. Recalculate in `03_Contracted_TCO_Lock.md`.
