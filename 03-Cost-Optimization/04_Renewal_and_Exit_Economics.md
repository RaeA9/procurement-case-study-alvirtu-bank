# Renewal and Exit Economics

**Document ID:** COST-XIT-004  
**Institution:** AlVirtu Bank (simulated)  
**Date:** 18 September 2026

---

Award savings die at renewal if the Bank cannot leave.

## 1. Renewal rule

Eighteen months before term-end:

1. Re-run TPRM-RSK-002 and Section B of the DDQ.
2. Re-price the book against the original 17.5% freeze — any proposed step-up needs a BATNA.
3. Do not roll auxiliary SKUs that duplicate the platform. That is ledger leakage, not growth.

A renewal that only “holds last year’s price” after seven years of inflation is a commercial win only if residency, SLA, and exit clauses stay intact.

## 2. Exit cash (order of magnitude)

| Item | Case treatment |
| :--- | :--- |
| Data extract | Open format within 30 days |
| Knowledge transfer | 90 days at no extra fee after material breach; otherwise a pre-priced pack |
| Escrow release | Triggered on insolvency, material breach, or failure to support |
| Parallel run | Budgeted in the exit paper; not a surprise SI change-order |
| Licence overlap | Avoid a second full core fee during extract; time-box dual running |

Detail sits in `06_Governance_and_Exit_Strategy/06_Vendor_Exit_Strategy_Cost_Analysis_AlVirtu.md`.

## 3. What not to do

- Waive escrow to “save” an annual fee.
- Let man-day float in years six and seven.
- Accept Dev/Test offshore “just for the exit factory.” That reopens the knock-out.
- Pay a new 22% maintenance book to the outgoing vendor to keep the lights on while the extract slips.
